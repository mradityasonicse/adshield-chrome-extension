# Privacy Policy for AdShield Pro

Last updated: November 2026

AdShield Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
AdShield Pro does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, IP addresses, or telemetry data.

All filtering decisions and statistics are processed strictly on your local device.

## 2. Permissions Justification
- `declarativeNetRequest` / `declarativeNetRequestFeedback`: Used exclusively to block network requests to known ad servers and tracking domains at the browser level.
- `storage`: Used solely on your local device to store user preferences (such as your custom whitelist and lifetime blocked count). No data is synced to any external server.
- `activeTab` / `tabs`: Used to detect the current website hostname for per-site whitelisting and to display the blocked counter badge.
- `<all_urls>`: Required for cosmetic DOM filtering (collapsing empty ad slots and hiding intrusive banners) across websites you visit.

## 3. Third-Party Services
AdShield Pro contains no third-party analytics, no tracking libraries, and makes no remote network connections.

## 4. Contact
For questions or support regarding AdShield Pro, please open an issue on the project repository.
